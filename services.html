AutomatedTechnologies

Candidate email, checked before it sends.

AutomatedTechnologies helps recruiting teams make sure automated candidate-facing email says what it is supposed to say — to the right person, at the right stage of the process. Two engagements, fixed scope, plain prices.

Start by email 313-306-3767

Services

Two engagements. One job: the wrong email never goes out.

01

Communications Audit & Safeguard Design

$2,500 · fixed fee

A review of your candidate-facing automated email flows — application confirmations, status updates, scheduling notices, and rejections — tracing where an erroneous send can originate: template logic, merge fields, stage triggers, and system handoffs. You receive a written findings report and a safeguard design: the pre-send checks, approvals, and verification steps that catch the wrong message before a candidate ever sees it.

02

Pre-Send Verification Retainer

$750/month · ongoing

Continuing verification of outbound candidate communications once safeguards are in place. Automated flows are checked against the safeguard design on a recurring basis, templates and triggers are re-verified when they change, and anything that would send wrong is flagged before it goes out.

Background

Why this practice exists.

This service grew out of a real incident: an automated rejection sent in error by a company’s own recruiting pipeline — corrected afterwards, but only after it had already reached the candidate. The system worked exactly as it was configured. Nobody had verified what it would actually say.

AutomatedTechnologies is the practice of Justin Spencer Smith, Lead Technologist. His background is endpoint and systems engineering — large device fleets in regulated finance and public education — work where verifying a change before it deploys is the whole job. These engagements apply the same discipline to the messages a hiring pipeline sends.
