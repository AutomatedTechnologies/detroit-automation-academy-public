How this guide was built

  • Nonpartisan by construction. The guide makes no endorsements and carries no candidate advocacy or selective emphasis. Every certified candidate gets the same card format and the same method: sourced claims, flagged gaps.
  • Sources or it didn't happen. Every claim links to its source. Where our research found no platform or record, the card says so instead of filling the gap.
  • Flagged, not hidden. Amber flags mark anything unverified or conflicting — a disputed ballot status, a single-sourced claim, a judgeship two sources describe differently.
  • Research date: . Races evolve. Verify your final ballot at the Michigan Secretary of State's voter information center before you vote.
