            Detroit || else™

            Every bus, live in 3D.

            83 routes and 10,583 stops drawn on the actual streets of Detroit — DDOT, SMART, and the QLINE streetcar — with live vehicle positions, published about every five minutes.

            How to read it

            Pillars are buses. Pylons are stops — taller pylon, more routes. Tap a bus for its route, destination, speed and data age, shown right beside it. Tap a stop for its routes and live buses.

            Service alerts on the map

            When an agency reports a disruption, the map shows it where it happens: the affected stretch of route is redrawn, bypassed stops are ringed, and the route chips, trip planner and stop cards state what changed in words. Every symbol pairs its color with a shape and a label — nothing here is told by color alone.

            • ✕No service — no buses on the route or the marked stretch. On the map the stretch is dimmed and overdrawn with a red-and-white dashed line and a ✕; the route chip carries a red ✕ badge.
            • ↪Detour — the route is running on a different path and may skip stops. The affected stretch is overdrawn with a red-and-white dashed line and a ↪; stops the bulletin names as bypassed pulse with amber rings.
            • ◑Reduced service — the route is running, but with fewer buses than scheduled. The route is dimmed on the map and its chip carries a hollow ◑ badge.
            • ⇄Stop moved — the boarding point for a stop has been relocated. The old stop is ringed in blue with a ⇄ on the map; the stop card gives the details in words.
            • ℹNotice — everything else: events, reminders, general information. Notices appear in words on the route chips and cards; they draw nothing on the map.

            Navigating the map

            No account. No tracking. Your location never leaves this device.

            About this map

            Real routes, real buses.

            Route shapes and stop positions come from the agencies’ published GTFS schedule data; the bus markers are live vehicle positions from the agencies’ own feeds, published about every five minutes. Zoom in close and the markers resolve into true-scale bus models — each DDOT bus is matched to its fleet model (dimensions and seating from the DDOT fleet roster) and oriented to its reported heading. It is the same live layer that powered the retired Forge Line page’s bus count — the Forge Line concept itself was modeled on the transit Detroit already runs.

            Route shapes & stops: DDOT, SMART, and QLINE GTFS. Bus dimensions & seating: DDOT fleet roster via CPTDB wiki. Street underlay: U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line 2025 (Wayne, Oakland & Macomb counties). Live data: DDOT via Clever Devices BusTime; SMART via Swiftly GTFS-Realtime; QLINE via its public GTFS-RT feed. Developers: see the transit data API.
