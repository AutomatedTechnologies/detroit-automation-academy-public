      37 routes and 5,120 stops drawn on the actual streets of Detroit — with live bus positions, refreshed every 60 seconds.

      How to read it

      Pillars are buses. Pylons are stops — taller pylon, more routes. Tap a bus for its route, destination, speed and data age, shown right beside it. Tap a stop for its routes and live buses.

      Navigating the map

      No account. No tracking. Your location never leaves this device.

      About this map

      Real routes, real buses.

      Route shapes and stop positions come from DDOT’s published GTFS schedule data; the bus markers are live vehicle positions from the agency’s own feed, refreshed every 60 seconds. Zoom in close and the markers resolve into true-scale bus models — each bus is matched to its fleet model (dimensions and seating from the DDOT fleet roster) and oriented to its reported heading. This is the same live layer behind the count on the Forge Line page — the Forge Line concept itself is modeled on the transit Detroit already runs.

      Route shapes & stops: DDOT GTFS. Bus dimensions & seating: DDOT fleet roster via CPTDB wiki. Street underlay: U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line 2025 (Wayne, Oakland & Macomb counties). Live data: DDOT via Clever Devices BusTime. SMART snapshots via the Transit API. Developers: see the transit data API.
