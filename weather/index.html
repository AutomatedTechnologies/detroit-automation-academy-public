How the four dimensions work

  • Longitude × latitude — the map itself; pan and zoom freely.
  • Pressure level — MSC GeoMet publishes each GDPS pressure level as its own WMS layer (verified in its GetCapabilities, 2026-11-26: there is no ELEVATION-dimension layer), so the selector switches the layer of one WMS request — surface (2 m), 850, 700, 500, 250 hPa.
  • Time — the slider sets the WMS TIME parameter. On the forecast axis it steps 3-hourly through the current GDPS run (+0 to +240 h, 81 steps). On the radar axis it steps through the exact times listed in nowCoast's capabilities (about every 4 minutes; the most recent 30 are loaded).

Data: Environment and Climate Change Canada, MSC GeoMet (GDPS 15 km) · NOAA nowCoast (NEXRAD base reflectivity mosaic) · NOAA National Weather Service, api.weather.gov (Michigan alerts, Detroit observation).
