Places

Sponsored place layers.

Branded layers on the map — coffee, food, venues, your locations — labeled "Sponsored" and off by default, so riders choose them. The layer is priced partnership inventory; the rate is set per layer, on request. Fees to AUTOMATEDTECHNOLOGIES.

Pass distribution

Passes, through the agencies.

Live Transit Detroit does not sell transit passes. Pass distribution partnerships are arranged by direct agreement with the transit agencies; if your organization wants to put passes in riders' hands through the platform — an employer, a school, an event — start the conversation and we will structure it with the agency. Agreements are with AUTOMATEDTECHNOLOGIES.

Start the conversation

Agencies

For transit agencies.

Data, display, and rider-information partnerships for transit agencies, on custom multi-year terms. The platform already runs on agency-published feeds, measured in the open — the same measurement is available to the agencies themselves. Contracting party: AUTOMATEDTECHNOLOGIES.

Talk agency partnership

The guardrails

Partnership buys placement. Never influence.

Partners do not review, approve, or shape coverage, data, or the map. There is no advertising on the map itself — ever. The Detroit Voter Guide carries no partnerships and no advertising; it is funded by no sponsor. We reserve the right to decline any partner that doesn't fit the mission.
