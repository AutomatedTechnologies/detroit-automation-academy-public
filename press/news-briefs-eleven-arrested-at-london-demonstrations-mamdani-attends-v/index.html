The Free Press · October 9, 2026 · By Chelsea Jacobson

News briefs: Eleven Arrested at London Demonstrations, Mamdani Attends Vigil

  • The week of publication marked the third anniversary of the October 7, 2023 attacks; the article states Hamas killed about 1,200 people, most of them civilians, and kidnapped 251 into Gaza.
  • Israel’s Ministry of Diaspora Affairs and Combating Antisemitism said more than 250 demonstrations against Israel were planned worldwide, including 75 on October 7 itself; that is the Ministry’s claim, not an independent count.
  • Eleven people were arrested at pro-Palestinian demonstrations in London, some outside the Board of Deputies of British Jews’ office; protesters chanted “stop killing babies” and “we will see you in The Hague,” per the linked reporting.
  • New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani attended an October 7 vigil in Union Square organized by “Israelis for Peace,” honoring those killed on October 7 and Palestinians killed in the subsequent war; protesters there booed him and chanted “Zohran, you can’t hide, we charge you with genocide.”
  • A report published this week says Rama Duwaji, described as First Lady of New York City, illustrated two bottles for Olive Odyssey, a luxury Palestinian olive oil brand; one bottle is priced at nearly $100, and she illustrated the bottle honoring Khalil Abu Osba, described in the piece as a militant who shot down a British plane.

The piece is paywalled; facts are from the article’s accessible portion. Demonstration figures are the Israeli ministry’s claim; London arrest details via the linked BBC reporting; the vigil and olive-oil details as reported in the article. Original headline: "TGIF: Nudity Is Optimal".

Removed: the columnist’s first-person framing and personal asides, the “protesters just want peace” characterization, the “genocide culprit” and “warmth of collectivism” framing of the Mamdani segment, the characterizations of the olive oil company’s marketing copy, and the subscription paywall pitch.
