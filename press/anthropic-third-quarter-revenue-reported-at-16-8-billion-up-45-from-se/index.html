Anthropic Third-Quarter Revenue Reported at $16.8 Billion, Up 45% From Second Quarter

  • Anthropic's third-quarter revenue was $16.8 billion, up 45% from the previous quarter, according to an investor who attended a pre-IPO meeting with Anthropic leadership in San Francisco on Wednesday, cited in the report.
  • Revenue was $4.7 billion in the first quarter and $11.6 billion in the second quarter, according to the same person, cited in the report.
  • Anthropic is targeting a valuation of over $2 trillion, according to the report.

Revenue figures via an investor who attended the meeting, cited in the original report. No statement from Anthropic is documented in the source. Original headline: "Anthropic's Q3 revenue surges 45% to nearly $17 billion".
