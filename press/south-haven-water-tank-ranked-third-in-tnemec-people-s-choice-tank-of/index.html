South Haven Water Tank Ranked Third in Tnemec People's Choice Tank of the Year Voting; Winner to Be Named October 19

  • Tnemec Company holds the People's Choice Tank of the Year contest annually; voters may submit one vote per active email address, according to the report.
  • The tank in South Haven is currently ranked third in the contest, behind Detroit Lakes, Minnesota, and Staunton, Virginia, according to the report.
  • The People's Choice winner will be named October 19; that winner will join 11 other finalists for the 2026 Tank of the Year, which a Tnemec committee plans to announce October 23, according to the report.

Contest figures and dates attributed to Tnemec Company as reported. Original headline: "Michigan water tower a favorite to win 'Tank of the Year'".

Removed: Characterization of tanks as favorites and lists of nominees from other states.
