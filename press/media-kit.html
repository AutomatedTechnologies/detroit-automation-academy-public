Fact sheet — Live Transit Detroit

Live Transit Detroit is the academy's live transit map and trip planner for the Detroit region. Its page describes it in these terms: “every DDOT bus live, and SMART buses live under license (display only), on a 3D map: all 83 routes and 10,583 stops drawn on the actual streets, with live vehicle positions published about every five minutes, including the QLINE streetcar.”

Open Live Transit Detroit
