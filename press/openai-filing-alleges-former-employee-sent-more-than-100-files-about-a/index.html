OpenAI Filing Alleges Former Employee Sent More Than 100 Files About an Unreleased Device to a Personal Account

  • A new OpenAI filing in its lawsuit with Apple alleges that an employee who left OpenAI for Apple sent more than 100 files about an unreleased product to a personal account.
  • The filing also alleges that the employee later made a presentation about the unreleased product to senior Apple leadership.

The claims above are OpenAI's allegations in a court filing, as reported by Reuters; no response from Apple or the employee is documented in the source.
