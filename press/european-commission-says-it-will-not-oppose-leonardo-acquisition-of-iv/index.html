European Commission Says It Will Not Oppose Leonardo Acquisition of Iveco Defence Unit

  • The European Commission said on Wednesday it would not oppose Leonardo's acquisition of Iveco's defence unit.
  • The Commission said the deal was compatible with the internal market and with the European Economic Area Agreement.
  • Iveco and Leonardo announced the €1.7 billion ($1.89 billion) deal at the end of July 2025.
  • The report states the sale of the defence business paved the way for an all-cash tender offer for Iveco's remaining business by India's Tata Motors.
