The Press

Just the Facts

Current reporting, distilled to what can be verified. Characterization, prediction, and unattributed assertion removed. Every fact below is traceable to its source.

Edition of October 14, 2026 · Sources linked per story

Method

  1. Facts only. Dates, votes, counts, named statements, and documented events. Nothing else.
  2. Attribution preserved. Claims made by a source are labeled as claims by that source — never laundered into unattributed fact.
  3. Characterization removed. Adjectives about motive, importance, or trajectory are cut unless they are direct quotes, in which case the speaker is named.
  4. Headlines are rewritten. Each card's headline states the documented event in neutral terms. The original headline is preserved verbatim in the card's attribution note.
  5. Both sides, or the absence noted. On contested matters, a documented response from the other party is included when the source reports one; when none is documented, the card says so.
  6. Multiple sources. Cards are drawn from The Free Press, Reuters, Bridge Michigan, and WDET. The outlet is named on every card.
  7. Paywalled pieces are distilled from their accessible portions only, and the card states that.
  8. Exclusions are logged. Anniversary and commemoration pieces, and stories yielding no independently verifiable fact, are not carried. Every skipped story is logged with its reason in press/processed.json in this site's public repository.
  9. Every story links its source so any fact can be checked in context.
