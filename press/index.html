Live Transit Detroit

Every DDOT bus live in 3D. SMART buses shown live under license, display only. QLINE streetcar, shown live.

Positions poll continuously and publish about every five minutes; the map dead-reckons between updates.

83 routes tracked, estate-wide: 37 DDOT + 45 SMART + 1 QLINE. The trip planner computes over the 82 DDOT and SMART routes; the QLINE is carried as live display, not as planner service. The media kit also records 10,583 stops drawn on the actual streets, as published on the Live Transit page.
