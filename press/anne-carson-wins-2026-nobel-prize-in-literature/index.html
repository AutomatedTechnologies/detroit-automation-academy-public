Anne Carson Wins 2026 Nobel Prize in Literature

  • On October 8, 2026, the Swedish Academy awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Literature to Anne Carson, a 76-year-old Canadian poet, essayist, and translator of classical Greek literature. The announcement was made in Stockholm by the Academy’s permanent secretary, Ingrid Carlberg.
  • The Academy’s citation recognizes her “for her bold and inventive oeuvre that, in playful dialogue with the classical tradition, has created new forms for contemporary literature.” The prize carries 12 million Swedish crowns (about $1.2 million).
  • Carson, born in Toronto in 1950, is widely known for her retranslations of Greek classics — including the Sappho translations in her 2002 collection If Not, Winter. She is the 19th woman to receive the literature prize and the first Canadian laureate since Alice Munro in 2013.
