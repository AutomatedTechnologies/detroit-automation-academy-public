IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva Addresses U.S. National Debt of More Than $40 Trillion at the Bangkok Autumn Meeting on Wednesday

  • IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva said on Wednesday at the IMF and World Bank autumn meeting in Bangkok that the United States and other wealthy nations need to tackle mounting debt levels, according to the Associated Press report.
  • Georgieva said U.S. leaders recognize that the federal deficit and national debt are too high, and that economic growth alone would not be enough to address a national debt of more than $40 trillion, according to the report.
  • Rodrigo Valdes, director of the IMF's Fiscal Affairs Department, said fiscal risks are rising and that a proper reaction is largely absent in many countries, according to the report.

Statements attributed to Georgieva and Valdes, as reported by the Associated Press. No U.S. government response is documented in the source. Original headline: "IMF chief urges the US and other wealthy nations to rein in debt to ease global economic pressure".

Removed: None beyond the statements reported.
