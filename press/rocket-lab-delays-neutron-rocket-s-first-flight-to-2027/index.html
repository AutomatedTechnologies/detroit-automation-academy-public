Rocket Lab Delays Neutron Rocket's First Flight to 2027

  • Rocket Lab said on Wednesday that the inaugural launch of its Neutron rocket was delayed until 2027, according to the report.
  • The company's shares fell about 6% in extended trading after the announcement, according to the report.
  • Rocket Lab had previously targeted Neutron's inaugural flight for the fourth quarter of 2026; in August, the company said it aimed to get the vehicle to the launch pad in the fourth quarter rather than launching it then, according to the report.
  • Neutron is a reusable medium-lift rocket, according to the report.
  • Last month, the company signed a multi-year launch agreement for 20 missions for its Electron launch vehicle with Tokyo-based Synspective, according to the report.
