Two Inkster Boys, Ages 6 and 4, Found Safe in Norwalk, Ohio, on Wednesday; Their Father Was Found Dead at the Scene, Police Say

  • Two boys, ages 6 and 4, were found safe in a cornfield in Norwalk, Ohio, in Huron County on Wednesday after being taken from Inkster, police said, according to the report.
  • Police say the father assaulted and restrained the children's mother during an incident and then left with the boys in her vehicle, according to the report.
  • The father was found dead at the scene, according to the report. Police initially responded to Rouge Park on Detroit's west side on October 14 at around 12 p.m. in connection with the case, according to the report.

Facts attributed to Inkster police, as reported. Original headline: "2 Inkster children rescued in Ohio after alleged parental kidnapping".

Removed: None beyond the facts attributed to police in the report.
