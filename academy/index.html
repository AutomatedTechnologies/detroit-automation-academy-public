About Justin Spencer Smith

Justin Spencer Smith is a technologist in Detroit, Michigan. He built Live Transit — the live map and trip planner now running for eight cities — writes The Signal, and founded the Detroit Automation Academy, which teaches robotics, drones, and automation to young builders. This domain is his personal site: the work, the writing, and the record, in one place.
