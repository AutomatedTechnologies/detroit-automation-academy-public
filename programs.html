Programs

Four tracks. Zero fluff.

Detroit Automation Academy exists for one reason: the young people of this city should be the ones building the automated future, not watching it arrive from somewhere else. We teach robotics, drones, and automation the way Detroit taught the world manufacturing — hands on, no shortcuts, pride in the work. Every student leaves with something they made move, fly, or think.

This academy has never received institutional funding. No grant, no sponsor, no check that was promised. It was built anyway — nights, weekends, borrowed space — and it is being finished on principle.

Built without funding. Finished on principle.

The tracks

Pick a track. Build the thing.

01

Robotics Cohorts

Seasonal build cohorts — design, wire, and program robots that compete and perform. Challenge-based learning from day one.

02

Drones

Flight fundamentals, safety, and real missions. Students learn the airspace rules and the engineering behind the rotors.

03

Automation & IoT

Sensors, controllers, and the logic that connects them. The same systems thinking that runs modern factories.

04

Apple Code + Create

App design and Swift development for young creators — run with the Michigan Engineering Zone. From idea to working prototype.
