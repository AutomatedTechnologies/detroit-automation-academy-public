FLAGSHIP · DISTRO

Software for Macs that refuse to die.

Distro is the Academy’s store and workshop: abandoned and aging Mac software, disassembled, scanned, and rebuilt to one standard — Academy branding, a security audit recorded on every bundle, and a floor of macOS High Sierra so the machines Detroit already owns stay useful.

Its emphasis is defensive craft: opsec, red team, cybersecurity, and incident response — taught and tooled for authorized use.
