Where to vote today

Tuesday, November 3, 2026 · Polls open 7 a.m.–8 p.m.

Enter your ZIP code — Wayne, Oakland, or Macomb County. Every Election Day polling location in that ZIP appears below.

A ZIP code covers several precincts, and your vote counts only at your assigned precinct’s location. Confirm your exact precinct at Michigan.gov/Vote or call the Detroit Department of Elections at 313-876-0190. If you are in line by 8 p.m., you can vote.

Sources: City of Detroit election precincts; Michigan Bureau of Elections 2026 precincts and Wayne County community clerk notices; Oakland County Elections; Macomb County Clerk polling locations, November 2026. River Rouge precincts do not appear in a published official list — verify those at Michigan.gov/Vote. Absentee ballots: return to your own clerk’s office or a drop box in your community by 8 p.m. today.
