About

This is Justin Spencer Smith’s personal site. The work collected here is his: the transit map and planner he built for Detroit and eight other cities, the publications he writes, the voter guide, and the academy he runs.

Detroit Automation Academy, in its own description, provides “robotics, drones, and automation training for Detroit’s young builders — from first circuit to first career.” Its programs are four tracks: Robotics Cohorts; Drones; Automation & IoT; Apple Code + Create (run with the Michigan Engineering Zone).

The homepage names the organizations the academy was built alongside: University of Michigan Center for Innovation; Qualcomm Thinkabit Lab; College for Creative Studies; Michigan Engineering Zone.

The academy states on its homepage: “This academy has never received institutional funding.”

Source: archived homepage and media kit fact sheet (organization), as restored in the V1 tree.
