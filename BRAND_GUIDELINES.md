# Brand Guidelines — Detroit Automation Academy

**Status: ACTIVE** | Last revised: 2026-10-22
Owner: Justin Spencer Smith, Lead Technologist, AutomatedTechnologies

This document states the governing brand standard for the public estate of
AUTOMATEDTECHNOLOGIES, doing business as Detroit Automation Academy. It replaces
the "EPOCH VII Agentic Theme" guidelines retired on 2026-10-22, which described a
system the site has never served.

## The system (as served)

- **Palette:** Midnight `#0C1116` · Forge `#E85D1A` · Amber `#FFB000` ·
  Concrete `#9AA0A6` · Paper `#F5F2EA` — as implemented in `styles.css`.
- **Type:** Space Grotesk (display) · Inter (body).
- **Mark:** the Forge D — `assets/logo.webp` on the site; the canonical vector
  logo suite (mark, AUTOMATEDTECHNOLOGIES wordmark, and lockups for Detroit
  Automation Academy, Live Transit Detroit, and The Signal) is the source of
  truth for new artwork.
- **Lockups in this repo:** `branding/DAA_Logo_Horizontal.svg` /
  `branding/DAA_Logo_Horizontal.png` (mirrored at
  `assets/DAA_Logo_Horizontal.svg`).

## Naming

- Legal name: **AUTOMATEDTECHNOLOGIES** (one word, all caps) in legal contexts.
- Legal pages and rights assertions: **AutomatedTechnologies d/b/a Detroit
  Automation Academy**.
- Display names: **Detroit Automation Academy**, **Live Transit Detroit**,
  **The Signal** — full names at first mention; "DAA" only after a full-name
  first mention. Never "Automated Technologies" (two words — a different,
  unrelated company).
- Route counts always state their basis: "83 routes estate-wide" or
  "82 routes (planner)".

## Voice

Plain, factual, sourced. The positioning line is carried verbatim or not at all:
"I build Detroit's transit information layer — every bus, live, on one map — and
I measure what the big apps miss."

## Logo rules

- Never stretch, recolor, outline, or place the mark on clashing backgrounds.
- Minimum digital size: 32 px (mark height); use the solid mark at 48 px and
  under.
- Clear space equals the height of the D's stem on all sides.
- The mark is Forge `#E85D1A` in every variant — it is never recolored.
