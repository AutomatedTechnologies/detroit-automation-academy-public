3. Where the data comes from

The schedule, route, and stop data shown in the application comes from published GTFS schedule files. The street underlay is U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line data. Live vehicle positions and service information shown in the application come from the agencies' own systems, through the following sources, as each source is in use:

  • DDOT vehicle positions and service bulletins: the Detroit Department of Transportation's BusTime system, operated by Clever Devices.
  • SMART vehicle positions: Swiftly, Inc.
  • QLINE vehicle positions: the QLINE's public GTFS-Realtime stream.

Agency and company names are used only to state where the data comes from. Nothing in the application or these terms implies that any agency or company endorses this service.
