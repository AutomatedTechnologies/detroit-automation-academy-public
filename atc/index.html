Detroit Metro Approach — you are the controller

The default view is LIVE: real aircraft position reports around Detroit Metro from the feed credited in the header. This card opens the training game — entering SIMULATION mode replaces the live display with simulated traffic. Nothing in either mode is transmitted to any real system.

SIMULATION — NOT FOR OPERATIONAL USE. A training game built on FAA JO 7110.65 phraseology and terminal separation minima (3 NM lateral / 1,000 ft vertical). Not affiliated with the FAA.

  • Arrivals check in at the edge fixes at an assigned cruise altitude. Vector and descend them, then clear them for the ILS runway 22L approach and for landing. No landing clearance by short final = go-around.
  • Departures wait on runway 22R. Clear them for takeoff; they climb to 5,000 ft (or your assigned altitude) and exit at their departure fix.
  • Select an aircraft by clicking it on the scope or in the flight strips. Issue altitude, heading, speed, and direct-to clearances from the control panel.
  • Separation: keep 3 NM and 1,000 ft between aircraft. ▲ CAUTION = predicted loss within about two minutes. ■ CONFLICT = separation is being lost right now — every loss is scored against you.
  • SRCL automation: inside the dashed 25 NM ring, aircraft are on the SRCL — the Academy's (fictional) proprietary Short-Range Coordination Link. With SRCL AUTO on, the link sequences arrivals on the last leg, issues approach, landing, and takeoff clearances by datalink (logged with the ⇅ SRCL marker) when — and only when — separation is assured, and holds or sends around traffic it cannot clear safely. Any manual clearance you issue to a linked aircraft suspends its automation until you re-engage it from the selected-aircraft panel. You always have the final word: the master toggle is in the header.
  • Keys: Tab cycle aircraft · A approach · L land · T takeoff · P pause · +/− sim rate.

SRCL — DAA Short-Range Coordination Link

—
Proprietary datalink (fictional, simulation only). Coverage: 25 NM around KDTW. Inside coverage the link automates last-leg arrival sequencing and takeoff release under the same JO 7110.65 minima; outside it, aircraft are voice-only. Datalink traffic is logged with the ⇅ SRCL marker. [A] on a data block = automation engaged · [M] = manual (automation suspended by a controller clearance).
