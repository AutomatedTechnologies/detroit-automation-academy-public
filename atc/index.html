Detroit Metro Approach — you are the controller

SIMULATION — NOT FOR OPERATIONAL USE. A training game built on FAA JO 7110.65 phraseology and terminal separation minima (3 NM lateral / 1,000 ft vertical). Not affiliated with the FAA.

  • Arrivals check in at the edge fixes at 10,000–12,000 ft. Vector and descend them, then clear them for the ILS runway 22L approach and for landing. No landing clearance by short final = go-around.
  • Departures wait on runway 22R. Clear them for takeoff; they climb to 5,000 ft (or your assigned altitude) and exit at their departure fix.
  • Select an aircraft by clicking it on the scope or in the flight strips. Issue altitude, heading, speed, and direct-to clearances from the control panel.
  • Separation: keep 3 NM and 1,000 ft between aircraft. ▲ CAUTION = predicted loss within about two minutes. ■ CONFLICT = separation is being lost right now — every loss is scored against you.
  • Keys: Tab cycle aircraft · A approach · L land · T takeoff · P pause · +/− sim rate.
