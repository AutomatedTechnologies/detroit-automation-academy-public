1. The service

Live Transit Detroit is a free public map and journey planner operated by AutomatedTechnologies d/b/a Detroit Automation Academy. It displays public transit stops, routes, schedules, and — while the live sources are operating — vehicle positions for the Detroit area, and it computes journeys from published schedule data. There is no account, no charge, and no advertising.

2. Your agreement

By using Live Transit Detroit, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the service. These terms govern the whole application, including every data source it displays.

3. Where the data comes from

The schedule, route, and stop data shown in the application comes from published GTFS schedule files. The street underlay is U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line data. Live vehicle positions and service information shown in the application come from the agencies' own systems, through the following sources, as each source is in use:

  • DDOT vehicle positions and service bulletins: the Detroit Department of Transportation's BusTime system, operated by Clever Devices.
  • SMART vehicle positions: Swiftly, Inc.
  • QLINE vehicle positions: the QLINE's public GTFS-Realtime stream.

Agency and company names are used only to state where the data comes from. Nothing in the application or these terms implies that any agency or company endorses this service.

Terms — Live Transit Austin

Who publishes this service

Live Transit Austin is published by AUTOMATEDTECHNOLOGIES d/b/a Detroit Automation Academy ("we", "us"). It is an independent service. It is not published by, affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Capital Metropolitan Transportation Authority (CapMetro).

The service

Live Transit Austin shows CapMetro vehicle positions, service alerts, and scheduled journeys on a map of the Austin region (Travis, Williamson and Hays counties, Texas). Riders never pay for Live Transit. There are no ads on the map. Live positions are republished from CapMetro's own feeds on a short cycle; a position is only as current as its timestamp, and scheduled journeys are computed from the published timetable — check the "updated" time on the page before relying on either.

Privacy — the short version

No account. No tracking. Your location never leaves this device. Live Transit Austin has no accounts and no sign-up. If you use the "Near me" feature, your location is used in your browser, on your device, to find your nearest stop; it is not sent to us or stored. Map preferences (which layers you show, whether the start sheet is collapsed) are stored only in your own browser.
