How a city joins

Every city runs on its own agency's published data, under a license that permits the service to compute with it — not just display it. A city is listed here only when its license permits the work and its live data has been measured, not taken from documentation.

Live Transit is published by AutomatedTechnologies. Riders never pay, in any city. There are no ads on any Live Transit map, ever.
