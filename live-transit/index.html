Live Transit — Cities

One platform, many cities. Every city runs on its own transit agency's published data. Detroit is the reference build — live map and trip planner. Philadelphia, Cincinnati, Denver + Aurora, Austin, Sacramento, San Diego and Columbus run live on the same platform — all eight cities from the expansion program are open.

Detroit LIVE

DDOT · SMART · QLINE — 83 routes — live map and trip planner

The reference instance: every DDOT bus live in 3D, SMART buses shown live under license (display only), QLINE streetcar, and a native trip planner that computes from the agencies' published timetables.

How a city joins

Every city runs on its own agency's published data, under a license that permits the service to compute with it — not just display it. Fifty municipalities were reviewed; eight cleared that bar outright. A city is listed here only when its license permits the work and its live data has been measured, not taken from documentation.

Live Transit is published by AutomatedTechnologies. Riders never pay, in any city. There are no ads on any Live Transit map, ever.
