Live Transit — Cities

One platform, many cities. Every city here runs on its own agency's published data, behind a license review that has to permit computation — not just display — before a single line of city code is written. Detroit is the reference build. Philadelphia joins it live today. Six more cities have cleared the license review and are in measured staging; their state is stated plainly, including what is blocking each one.

San Diego STAGING

San Diego MTS — 104 routes · 4,359 stops

State: static feed validated; the realtime feed is key-gated and the key was requested from MTS on Oct 22 (the agency states 5–7 business days). The live page waits on that key — nothing else blocks it.

Columbus STAGING

COTA — 43 routes · 2,977 stops

State: static feed validated and the realtime feed answers on schedule, but its vehicle envelopes have arrived empty in every sample so far (evening and overnight; a peak-hours sample is scheduled). A live map with no vehicles on it would mislead, so the page waits on a populated feed.

Austin STAGING

Denver + Aurora STAGING

Sacramento STAGING

SacRT — 64 routes · 2,822 stops

State: technical validation complete — keyless feeds, via the agency portal's current static feed. (The catalog's older static link serves an expired feed; it is recorded as stale and is not used.)

Cincinnati STAGING

Metro (SORTA) — 53 routes · 3,513 stops

State: technical validation complete — every realtime endpoint keyless, and the best-measured feed in the program: 100% of vehicle records carry a trip assignment.

How a city gets here

Each municipality passes the same staging pipeline, in public: A0 catalog discovery → A1 license review (the terms must permit display and computation; display-only or non-commercial terms fail) → A2 technical validation, with cadence and coverage measured, never taken from documentation → A3 key custody → A4 basemap from authoritative geometry. Fifty municipalities were reviewed: 8 passed the license review outright, 20 are conditional, and 22 remain unresolved — none of those 42 is listed as a candidate until its license permits the work.
