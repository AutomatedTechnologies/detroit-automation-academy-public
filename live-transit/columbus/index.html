What you are looking at

The map covers the COTA service area — Columbus and Franklin County, reaching into southern Delaware County — drawn from U.S. Census TIGER/Line 2025 roads. Route lines and stops come from COTA's published GTFS. Service alerts come from COTA's agency-direct GTFS-Realtime alerts feed, fetched and republished by AutomatedTechnologies. COTA's vehicle-position and trip-update feeds publish no data — they return empty in every sampling — so this page presents scheduled service only and labels it that way.
