Who publishes this service

Live Transit Columbus is published by AUTOMATEDTECHNOLOGIES d/b/a Detroit Automation Academy ("we", "us"). It is an independent service. It is not published by, affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Central Ohio Transit Authority (COTA).

The service

Live Transit Columbus shows the COTA bus network, current service alerts, and scheduled journeys on a map of Columbus and Franklin County, Ohio (the COTA service area). Riders never pay for Live Transit. There are no ads on the map. COTA does not publish live vehicle positions or trip updates — its vehicle-position and trip-update feeds return empty — so this service presents scheduled service only: times are the published timetable's, not a bus's actual location. Service alerts are republished from COTA's own alerts feed.

Privacy — the short version

No account. No tracking. Your location never leaves this device. Live Transit Columbus has no accounts and no sign-up. If you use the "Near me" feature, your location is used in your browser, on your device, to find your nearest stop; it is not sent to us or stored. Map preferences (which layers you show, whether the start sheet is collapsed) are stored only in your own browser.
