Terms — Live Transit Denver & Aurora

Who publishes this service

Live Transit Denver & Aurora is published by AUTOMATEDTECHNOLOGIES d/b/a Detroit Automation Academy ("we", "us"). It is an independent service. It is not published by, affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Regional Transportation District (RTD).

The service

Live Transit Denver & Aurora shows RTD vehicle positions, service alerts, and scheduled journeys on a map of the Denver region (the RTD district: Denver, Adams, Arapahoe, Jefferson, Boulder, Douglas and Broomfield counties, Colorado). Riders never pay for Live Transit. There are no ads on the map. Live positions are republished from RTD's own feeds on a short cycle; a position is only as current as its timestamp, and scheduled journeys are computed from the published timetable — check the "updated" time on the page before relying on either.

Privacy — the short version

No account. No tracking. Your location never leaves this device. Live Transit Denver & Aurora has no accounts and no sign-up. If you use the "Near me" feature, your location is used in your browser, on your device, to find your nearest stop; it is not sent to us or stored. Map preferences (which layers you show, whether the start sheet is collapsed) are stored only in your own browser.
