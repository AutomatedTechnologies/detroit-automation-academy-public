How to read these results

  • Each card is a distinct way to make the trip — fastest, fewest transfers, least walking, direct, or the journey that best survives a missed connection on the published schedule. A card can be several of those at once.
  • “Most reliable (scheduled)” is about the timetable: how much room the schedule leaves to recover. It is not measured on-time performance, and this planner does not use live vehicle positions.
  • The timetable covers SEPTA's bus feed to February 20, 2027 and Regional Rail to November 21, 2026. Trips are computed from stops — pick the exact stop you will board at; stops that share a name are listed separately.
