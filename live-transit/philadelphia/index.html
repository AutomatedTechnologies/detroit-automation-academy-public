Philadelphia — Live Transit

SEPTA buses, trolleys, the subway and Regional Rail, live on one map. Data comes from SEPTA's own published feeds. Positions on this page are republished about every five minutes; SEPTA's feeds themselves update every 30–60 seconds. There are no ads on this map. Ever.

What you are looking at

The map covers the five-county Pennsylvania SEPTA region (Philadelphia, Bucks, Chester, Delaware and Montgomery counties), drawn from U.S. Census TIGER/Line 2025 roads. Route lines and stops come from SEPTA's published GTFS (feed version v202609271): 178 routes with scheduled service and 13,665 stops. Vehicle positions come from SEPTA's agency-direct GTFS-Realtime feeds, fetched and republished by AutomatedTechnologies — the same publish pattern as Live Transit Detroit.

Read the data honestly

  • A vehicle drawn in its route's color carries a route assignment in SEPTA's feed. A gray vehicle is reporting a position without one — about half of SEPTA's bus vehicle records carry a route assignment at any given time. Nothing here is inferred or filled in.
  • Regional Rail reaches Trenton (NJ) and Wilmington and Newark (DE). Street geometry for New Jersey and Delaware is not yet staged, so those stations — and any vehicle outside the mapped area — are not drawn, rather than drawn over blank ground.
  • Individual vehicles report at varying rates inside a fresh feed; the status line above shows each feed's own timestamp, and a tapped vehicle shows the time of its own last report.

About this service

Live Transit Philadelphia is published by AutomatedTechnologies d/b/a Detroit Automation Academy, from SEPTA's public data under SEPTA's developer license (reviewed and recorded by the program before build). This page makes no use of SEPTA branding and implies no endorsement. Riders never pay for Live Transit. Trip planning is on the way for Philadelphia. Until it lands, this map is the service: every live vehicle, every route, every stop.
