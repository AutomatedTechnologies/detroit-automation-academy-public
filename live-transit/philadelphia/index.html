What you are looking at

The map covers the five-county Pennsylvania SEPTA region (Philadelphia, Bucks, Chester, Delaware and Montgomery counties), drawn from U.S. Census TIGER/Line 2025 roads. Route lines and stops come from SEPTA's published GTFS. Vehicle positions come from SEPTA's agency-direct GTFS-Realtime feeds, fetched and republished by AutomatedTechnologies.
