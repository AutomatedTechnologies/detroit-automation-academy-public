Terms — Live Transit Sacramento

Who publishes this service

Live Transit Sacramento is published by AUTOMATEDTECHNOLOGIES d/b/a Detroit Automation Academy ("we", "us"). It is an independent service. It is not published by, affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Sacramento Regional Transit District (SacRT).

The service

Live Transit Sacramento shows SacRT vehicle positions, service alerts, and scheduled journeys on a map of Sacramento County, California (the SacRT service area). Riders never pay for Live Transit. There are no ads on the map. Live positions are republished from SacRT's own feeds on a short cycle; a position is only as current as its timestamp, and scheduled journeys are computed from the published timetable — check the "updated" time on the page before relying on either.

Privacy — the short version

No account. No tracking. Your location never leaves this device. Live Transit Sacramento has no accounts and no sign-up. If you use the "Near me" feature, your location is used in your browser, on your device, to find your nearest stop; it is not sent to us or stored. Map preferences (which layers you show, whether the start sheet is collapsed) are stored only in your own browser.

SacRT data and the SacRT GTFS terms

The transit data on this page — routes, stops, schedules, vehicle positions, trip updates, and service alerts — is SacRT Data obtained from SacRT's transit data portal (apps.sacrt.com) and its realtime endpoints (bustime.sacrt.com), and used under the SacRT GTFS terms, which govern both the schedule and the realtime data.   • The data remains SacRT's. Schedules and real-time information are SacRT's to publish and correct; where this page and SacRT's own channels disagree, SacRT's channels govern.

Street geometry is drawn from U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line 2025 data (public domain). Landmark footprints come from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
